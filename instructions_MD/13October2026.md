Task creation never depends on the email: a delivery failure shows a banner (`Task created, but the notification email could not be sent: ...`) and the success notice adds `(email sent)`.
- Server-side checks (the caller's JWT is verified, then the service role is used): only the task's creator can trigger it, only for tasks created in the last 15 minutes, the caller must be an active owner/admin/editor of the task's organisation, the assignee must be an active member of that organisation, and nothing is sent when you assign a task to yourself.
